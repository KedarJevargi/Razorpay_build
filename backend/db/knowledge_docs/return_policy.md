# Return & Refund Policy
## 30-Day Returns
We offer a 30-day return policy for most unused gear in its original packaging with all tags attached. 

## Exceptions
- Safety gear (climbing ropes, harnesses, helmets) cannot be returned due to safety regulations.
- Clearance items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

## Refund Process
Once we receive the returned item, it takes 2-3 business days to inspect it. Upon approval, the refund will be initiated to your original payment method via Razorpay. It may take 5-7 business days for the amount to reflect in your account.
