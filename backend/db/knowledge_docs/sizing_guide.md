# Sizing & Fit Guide

## Backpack Sizing
Choosing the right backpack depends on your torso length, not your height.

### How to Measure Your Torso
1. Tilt your head forward and feel for the bony bump where your neck meets your shoulders — that's your C7 vertebra.
2. Place your hands on your hips with thumbs pointing backward. Draw an imaginary line between your thumbs — that's your iliac crest.
3. Measure from C7 down to the iliac crest. This is your torso length.

### Torso Length to Pack Size
| Torso Length | Frame Size |
|-------------|-----------|
| Up to 40cm | Extra Small |
| 40–45cm | Small |
| 45–50cm | Medium |
| 50cm+ | Large |

### Which Capacity Do I Need?
| Trip Duration | Recommended Capacity |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Day hike (4–8 hours) | 15L – 25L (UltraLight 20L, Women's DayPack 25L) |
| Overnight / 1-2 nights | 30L – 40L (TrailPack 30L, SummitPro 40L) |
| Multi-day trek (3-5 days) | 40L – 55L (SummitPro 40L) |
| Extended expedition (6+ days) | 55L+ (Expedition 60L) |

## Sleeping Bag Temperature Ratings
Our sleeping bags use the EN 13537 standard:
- **Comfort Rating**: Temperature at which a standard woman can sleep comfortably.
- **Lower Limit**: Temperature at which a standard man can sleep for 8 hours without waking.
- **Extreme Rating**: Survival-only; risk of hypothermia.

| Product | Comfort | Lower Limit | Extreme |
|---------|---------|-------------|---------|
| CompactSleep Bag 0°C | 5°C | 0°C | -15°C |
| UltraWarm Bag -10°C | -4°C | -10°C | -30°C |

**Tip**: Add a Silk Sleeping Liner for 5-8°C extra warmth.

## Tent Capacity
Tent person ratings are based on a snug fit. For comfortable camping, we recommend sizing up:
- **Solo trekker**: 1-person tent (or 2-person for comfort)
- **Couple**: 2-person tent (or 4-person for comfort + gear storage)
- **Family of 3-4**: 4-person tent

## Boot Sizing
Our boots run true to Indian sizing. For trekking boots, we recommend going half a size up from your regular shoe size to allow for thick hiking socks and foot swelling on long trails.
