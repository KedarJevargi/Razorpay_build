# Gear Care & Maintenance

## Backpack Care
- **Cleaning**: Hand wash with lukewarm water and mild soap. Never machine wash — it can damage the waterproof coating and buckles.
- **Drying**: Air dry in shade. Never tumble dry or expose to direct sunlight for prolonged periods.
- **Storage**: Store uncompressed in a cool, dry place. Stuff sacks compress foam padding and reduce its lifespan.
- **Zippers**: Apply silicone lubricant to zippers every few months to keep them running smoothly.

## Tent Maintenance
- **After every trip**: Shake out debris, wipe down with a damp cloth, and air dry completely before packing.
- **Re-waterproofing**: Apply seam sealer every 1-2 seasons. Use DWR spray on the fly when water stops beading.
- **Pole care**: Wipe aluminum poles with a dry cloth. If poles stick, clean joints with rubbing alcohol.
- **Storage**: Store loosely rolled (not stuff-sacked) to prevent creasing the waterproof coating.

## Sleeping Bag Care
- **Synthetic bags**: Machine wash on gentle/cold cycle with technical wash. Tumble dry low with tennis balls to restore loft.
- **Down bags**: Use down-specific wash only (e.g., Nikwax Down Wash). Never use regular detergent — it strips the natural oils.
- **Storage**: Always store in a large cotton or mesh storage sack — never in the compression sack.
- **Freshening up**: Air out after every trip. Spot clean with a damp cloth for small stains.

## Footwear Care
- **After muddy hikes**: Remove insoles, rinse under cold water, stuff with newspaper and air dry.
- **Leather boots**: Apply leather conditioner every 3-4 months. Re-apply DWR spray for waterproofing.
- **Never dry near heat**: Campfires, heaters, and direct sunlight damage adhesives and warp soles.

## Water Filter Maintenance
- **Backflushing**: Backflush the PureStream filter after every trip to extend filter life.
- **Winter use**: Never allow the filter to freeze — ice crystals damage the filter membrane permanently.
- **Replacement**: Replace the filter cartridge after 4000L or when flow rate drops significantly.
