# Frequently Asked Questions

## Which backpack size do I need?
For a day hike, a 15L-30L backpack is usually sufficient. For a multi-day trek (2-3 days), we recommend a 40L-50L backpack. For longer expeditions, 60L+ is recommended.

## Do you offer a warranty?
Yes, all our backpacks and tents come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. It does not cover wear and tear from normal use.

## Are your tents waterproof?
All our tents have a minimum hydrostatic head rating of 2000mm, which is considered fully waterproof for most rainy conditions.
